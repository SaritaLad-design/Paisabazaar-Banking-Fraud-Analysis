# Paisabazaar Banking Fraud & Credit Risk Analysis

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing customer banking and credit-related data to identify risky financial behavior, detect potential fraud patterns, and understand factors affecting customer credit scores.

The analysis was performed using Python, SQL, Matplotlib, and Seaborn for data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), and visualization.

The project helps financial institutions and fintech companies like Paisabazaar make data-driven decisions related to credit risk assessment, loan approvals, and fraud monitoring.

---

## Business Objective
The main objectives of this project are:

- Analyze customer financial behavior
- Identify high-risk customers
- Understand factors affecting credit scores
- Detect risky loan and payment patterns
- Improve fraud and credit risk monitoring
- Support better financial decision-making

## Key Analysis Performed

### Credit Score Analysis
- Analyzed distribution of customer credit scores
- Identified factors impacting good and poor credit scores

### Loan & Debt Analysis
- Studied relationship between loans, EMI, and outstanding debt
- Identified customers with high financial liabilities

### Payment Behavior Analysis
- Examined delayed payments and due date delays
- Analyzed payment habits affecting creditworthiness

### Income & Financial Stability Analysis
- Compared annual income with monthly balance and investments
- Studied customer financial health patterns

### Credit Utilization Analysis
- Analyzed how credit utilization affects credit scores
- Identified risky utilization behavior

### Banking Risk Analysis
- Detected patterns indicating financial risk and possible fraud behavior

## Key Insights
- Customers with higher delayed payments tend to have poor credit scores
- High outstanding debt negatively impacts financial stability
- Customers with balanced credit utilization generally maintain better credit scores
- Excessive loans and credit inquiries increase financial risk
- Payment behavior plays a major role in creditworthiness
